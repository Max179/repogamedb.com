## Rules applied
- One hero picture per entry; the original SVG diagram stays as the second image and is labelled "not a screenshot".
- A picture whose subject is not literal carries `illustrative: true` and a `shows` description, and the page
  caption says what the picture actually shows.
- Community pictures are recorded in `content/images-manifest.json` with source page, author, licence and retrieval
  date; they are listed on `/credits.html`.
- Reused files keep one manifest record and one credits row; reuse happens only when the subject is the same or the
  picture is explicitly illustrative.
- For the six entries with no depictable subject (glossary, FAQ, camera, orb, debug, version) the starter picture is
  kept only because it is explicitly labelled illustrative; a better Fandom picture can replace it without changing
  the pipeline.
