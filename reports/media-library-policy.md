# R.E.P.O. media library policy

## UI artwork

`web/assets/generated/` contains original model-generated artwork used only for the site shell: the hero banner and four category badges. These files are not presented as gameplay screenshots or entity evidence.

## Wiki artwork

Published entity pages may use one of three sources:

1. readable exports from the user-owned Windows build, with the source path recorded in `web/assets/media-manifest.json`;
2. public-domain or explicitly reusable community art, with the license and attribution recorded before import;
3. a remote reference link to a community wiki when reuse permission is not established.

Fandom and competitor wikis are useful for fact checking and asset discovery, but their visible images are not automatically reusable. Game screenshots and extracted art remain rightsholder material unless a license or user-owned reuse boundary is recorded. We do not hotlink or silently copy those images.

The Windows image-mapping audit is the next source for one-to-one entity images. Until that sync is available, editorial illustrations are labeled as illustrations and are not described as entity-specific screenshots.
